📊 Blinkit Sales Dashboard (Power BI)
1. Project Title

Blinkit Sales Dashboard – Interactive Business Intelligence Analysis Using Power BI

2. Project Overview

The Blinkit Sales Dashboard is an interactive Business Intelligence (BI) solution developed in Microsoft Power BI to analyze sales performance and business trends. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales, customer purchasing behavior, product performance, payment methods, and regional sales distribution.

Using interactive filters and KPIs, users can quickly explore business data, identify trends, and make informed decisions. The project demonstrates practical skills in data cleaning, transformation, visualization, and dashboard design.

4. Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large amounts of transactional data every day. Without proper visualization and analysis, it becomes difficult to:

Monitor sales performance
Identify high-performing products
Track customer purchasing behavior
Compare regional sales
Analyze payment preferences
Support strategic business decisions

This dashboard addresses these challenges by transforming raw sales data into meaningful visual insights.

5. Objectives
Analyze overall sales performance.
Monitor monthly sales trends.
Compare sales across different brands.
Identify top-performing areas.
Analyze customer payment methods.
Track order quantity and average order value.
Build an interactive dashboard for business users.

Cash was the most preferred payment method.
Royal Brand generated the highest sales.
Rajahmundry contributed the highest regional sales.
Dairy & Bakery products showed strong performance.
The average order value remained approximately ₹1.37K.
